Magens Bay

Classic wide crescent; busy on multi-ship days. Allow more transfer time from Crown Bay. See beach excursions.

Sapphire Beach

East-end resort shoreline. Related: private Sapphire Beach tour.

Coki Beach

Pocket beach often chosen for near-shore snorkelling. Also see snorkelling tours.

Secret Harbour

Calmer cove feel; confirm logistics. Private tours can bundle this flexibly.

Which St Thomas beach is right for you?

  • Wide swim: Magens Bay.
  • Resort east end: Sapphire Beach.
  • Near-shore snorkel: Coki Beach.
  • Calmer cove: Secret Harbour.
  • Short call: Protect the return buffer — see one-day scenarios.
