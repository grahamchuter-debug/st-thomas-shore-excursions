St Thomas cruise calls centre on Havensight and Crown Bay. Charlotte Amalie is the harbour town, but transfer time depends on which facility your ship uses.

Havensight

Near-town cruise complex for shopping and taxi access. Allow time on multi-ship days.

Crown Bay

Further west — Magens, Sapphire and Coki transfers need more honest clock time.

Charlotte Amalie relationship

Short hop from Havensight; deliberate transfer from Crown Bay.

Transport and return planning
