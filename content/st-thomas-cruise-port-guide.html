St Thomas cruise calls centre on Havensight and Crown Bay. Charlotte Amalie is the harbour town many guests associate with the island, but walking distance and transfer time depend on which facility your ship uses.

Connect to one-day plans, beaches and shore excursions. We do not invent berth assignments or sailing times.

Havensight

Often the more familiar near-town cruise complex for shopping and taxi access. Allow time on multi-ship days.

Crown Bay

Further west. Beach transfers to Magens, Sapphire or Coki generally need more honest clock time than from Havensight.

Charlotte Amalie relationship

From Havensight it is a short hop; from Crown Bay it is a deliberate transfer.

Transport and return planning

  • Confirm tour meeting points.
  • Independent taxis: agree destination and return before leaving the port area.
  • Prefer plans you can exit early.
