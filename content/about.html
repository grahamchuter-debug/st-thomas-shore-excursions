About St Thomas Shore Excursions

This site is an independent planning guide for cruise passengers calling at St Thomas in the US Virgin Islands. We help you compare beaches, snorkelling, sightseeing and private touring options against a realistic port day from Havensight or Crown Bay.

We do not operate tours, sell tickets or take payment on this website in this phase. If you book, you book with operators you choose separately. We are not affiliated with any cruise line.

We do not claim a physical office on St Thomas. Guidance here is editorial research for cruise passengers, not a local concierge desk.

See our methodology for how recommendations are framed, and contact for how to reach us.
