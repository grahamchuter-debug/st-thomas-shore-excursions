A cruise day shaped by
Havensight or Crown Bay

St Thomas rewards clear priorities. Most ships use Havensight (near Charlotte Amalie) or Crown Bay further west. Your berth affects transfer time to Magens Bay, Sapphire, Coki and the east-end beaches.

This guide stays editorial — beach days, snorkelling, sightseeing and private touring without fake availability or ratings.
