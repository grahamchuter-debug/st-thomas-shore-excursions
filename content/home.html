US Virgin Islands

A cruise day shaped by
Havensight or Crown Bay

St Thomas rewards clear priorities. Most ships use Havensight (near Charlotte Amalie) or Crown Bay further west. Your berth affects transfer time to Magens Bay, Sapphire, Coki and the east-end beaches, so start with port orientation before you lock a tour style.

This guide stays editorial. We compare beach days, snorkelling, sightseeing mixes and private touring so you can choose calmly — without fake availability, ratings or urgency.
