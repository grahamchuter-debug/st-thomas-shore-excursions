Which St Thomas beach is right for you?

  • Classic wide swim: Magens Bay, if transfers fit.
  • Resort-side east end: Sapphire Beach.
  • Near-shore snorkel interest: Coki Beach — or a dedicated snorkel trip.
  • Calmer cove: Secret Harbour with confirmed logistics.
